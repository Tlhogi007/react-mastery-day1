# HOW TO POST TO MEDIUM - STEP BY STEP

## 1. Open Medium
Go to: https://medium.com/new-story

## 2. Copy the Content
Open this file: MEDIUM_READY.md
Copy everything under "Article Content"

## 3. Paste into Medium
Paste into the Medium editor

## 4. Add Title & Subtitle
At the top of the page:
- Title: "I Built an Interactive Platform to Master React Internals in 30 Days"
- Subtitle: "From setState to Fiber — a structured learning path for ambitious engineers who want to understand, not just use React"

## 5. Add Screenshots
Click the "+" button on the left to add images at these locations:

**Image 1:** After "What I Built" section
- File: screenshots/landing/01-hero.png
- Caption: "The landing page with animated terminal showing common React mistakes"

**Image 2:** After "Interactive Learning" section
- File: screenshots/landing/02-concepts.png
- Caption: "Interactive concept cards with visual representations of React internals"

**Image 3:** After "The Technical Stack" section
- File: screenshots/app/01-dashboard.png
- Caption: "The main dashboard with progress tracking and navigation"

**Image 4:** After "What Makes This Different" section
- File: screenshots/landing/03-comparison.png
- Caption: "What makes this different from typical React courses"

**Image 5:** After "Early Results" section
- File: screenshots/app/02-day1-content.png
- Caption: "Day 1 content with interactive diagrams and code examples"

## 6. Set Cover Image
- Click "Add a cover image" at the top
- Upload: screenshots/landing/01-hero.png

## 7. Add Tags
Click "Add tags" at the bottom:
1. React
2. JavaScript
3. Web Development
4. Programming
5. Software Engineering

## 8. Add Your Links
Replace "[Add your Twitter/LinkedIn/GitHub links here]" with your actual links

## 9. Preview
Click the eye icon to preview how it looks

## 10. Publish
- Click "Publish" button (top right)
- Choose "Public" visibility
- Click "Publish now"

## DONE! 🎉

After publishing:
- Copy the Medium URL
- Share on Twitter, LinkedIn, Reddit
- Respond to comments

---

**Need help?** Just follow these steps one by one. It takes about 10 minutes.
